## How to read this

Two caveats worth holding onto:

1. **A high count means the requirement is common, not that it is essential.** Kubernetes appears in half of all postings partly because it is boilerplate in infrastructure job descriptions.
2. **Add nothing here you cannot defend in an interview.** The scorer strips skills it cannot evidence from your resume and re-derives the score downward, so padding this list does not raise your match - it just gets removed. The only durable way to move these numbers is to genuinely have the experience, or to surface experience you already have but never wrote down.

The second category above is where the free points are.
