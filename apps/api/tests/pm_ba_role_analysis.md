## 7. Caveats & known limits

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **USAJOBS** | Free‑tier API requires a registered key (not in `.env`). No federal/defense PM volume included; add the key if needed. |
| **Resume‑corpus overlap** | The “not‑detected” list from earlier analysis ran against the **profile KV only**; actual resume text files were not opened. Positive skill detections are verified; gaps should be confirmed against your real resume. |
| **Source coverage** | Only permitted free APIs were pulled (see methodology). LinkedIn/Indeed/paid feeds are intentionally excluded per the #96 research verdicts. |
| **PM‑only AI exposure** | AI‑GenAI appears in only ~8 % of PM JDs and ~4 % of BA/DA/BIE/IMPL JDs — the market for pure AI‑product roles is still a minority in the permitted‑source feed set. |

---

## 8. How to use this

- **Resume‑skill prioritisation:** Focus on the top‑3 skills per sub‑bucket that are *not* already heavily represented in your current resume (e.g., if you lack “Stakeholder management” or “SQL”, those are the highest‑leverage additions).
- **Target‑role routing:** The `/application-assistant/autopilot/jobs` endpoint now defaults review/manual‑review buckets to `sortBy=relevance` — the most relevant roles to your target profiles (Senior AI Engineer, Lead AI Engineer, AI Automation Engineer, AI PM, BA/DA/BIE) surface first.
- **Skill‑gap courses:** The skill lists above map cleanly to popular online‑learning categories (SQL, Tableau, Agile, Jira, stakeholder‑mgmt workshops, product‑strategy frameworks).
